---
name: SEO audit triage
description: How to interpret automated SEO recommendations without degrading page intent or privacy.
---

Automated word-count warnings on contact and policy pages are not instructions to pad them. Filtered catalog states may intentionally canonicalize to a single unfiltered page; a crawler's non-self-canonical notice does not by itself justify making each filter indexable. Title length is an editorial guard, not a Google ranking threshold.

**Why:** The owner wants useful visitor-facing copy without unnecessary business identifiers, and the external crawl combines actionable metadata warnings with informational notices and generic growth ideas. Inflating policy text or inventing search demand just to clear a report would reduce accuracy.

**How to apply:** Validate each recommendation against live behavior and page intent. Keep legal and cookie text concise and factual, retain deliberate deduplication, and use actual Search Console query/link data before committing to keyword clusters or backlink outreach.
